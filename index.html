Past cycles

Main-conference research tracks only. Every date read from the venue's own call for papers or HotCRP, then re-checked against the same source. Timezones are per-date — MICRO publishes EDT, not AoE. Where a venue publishes no clock time the counter runs to end of day and says so; it is never invented. Closed cycles sort to the bottom. The countdown is timezone-independent — a duration is the same everywhere, so the selector only changes which calendar date is printed. All deadlines live in data.json.

On the estimates. A dashed circle means the call for papers is not out and the countdown is that venue's last cycle shifted a year. Tested against the three venues here whose next cycle has since been published, that rule landed 1 day, 6 days and 20 days from the real date — and in the two larger cases the real deadline came later than the estimate. Treat an estimate as accurate to a few weeks, in the safe direction. Venue timing also moves in ways arithmetic cannot follow: HPCA 2027 sits seven weeks later in the calendar than HPCA 2026 while its submission deadline barely moved, and DATE compressed its notification-to-camera-ready gap from about nine weeks to three.
